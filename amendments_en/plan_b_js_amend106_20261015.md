# Pre-Registration Amendment 106: Post-Hoc Confirmation That the 4 Unmeasurable Models Were Not "Easy-to-Pass Models"

**Created**: 2026-10-15
**Target**: Amendments 97 and 98 (selection rule and unmeasurable models for the activation-cluster control)
**Type**: **Post-hoc confirmation; not pre-registered.** The judgement criteria, population, and numbers are not changed in any way.

---

## CY1. Question

The activation-cluster control was unmeasurable for 4 of 9 models, and the unmeasurable models are those with higher emotion partition decodability. **The suspicion remains: "Were the 5 measurable models inherently easy-to-pass models?"** (Already stated explicitly as a limitation in Amendment 98.)

## CY2. Post-Hoc Confirmation

Gate II has another control **measurable for all 9 models** — the 100 random partitions.
If the 4 unmeasurable models were "weak" models, their performance should be inferior on this control as well.

| Group | Median count | Range | Median excess SD |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 models that were unmeasurable | 8.5/13 | 6–11 | +2.06 |
| 5 models for which judgement was possible | 8.0/13 | 6–10 | +2.81 |

**Indistinguishable.** Ranges overlap, and the unmeasurable group actually has the higher median count.

## CY3. What Determines Unmeasurability

The split is clean on the margin left by the selection rule (candidate partition AUC upper bound − emotion partition AUC).

| Group | Margin |
|---|---|
| 4 unmeasurable models | **+0.0015 to +0.0034** |
| 5 models for which judgement was possible | **+0.0077 to +0.0327** |

**Unmeasurability is determined not by the strength of the effect but by the relationship between the upper bound of activation clustering and the position of the emotion partition.**

## CY4. What Can and Cannot Be Said

- Can say: **The two groups are indistinguishable on the random-partition control.** Therefore the explanation "the models that were testable were easy-to-pass models" is not supported, at least by this control.
- Cannot say: **It is not refuted.** The selection is correlated with decodability, and performance on random partitions does not directly verify that correlation. The limitation statement is not retracted.

## CY5. Incorporation into Body Text

Added to the limitation (third point): the margin figures and the comparison of both groups, positioned as **"evidence against the selection, not a refutation."** The limitation itself remains.
