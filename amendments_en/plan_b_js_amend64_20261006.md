## BM1. Framing of Primary Results (The Strongest Form Supported by the Data in Hand)

Under the same model, the same directions, the same floor, and the same perturbation budget, two measurements are in hand that differ **only in the readout**.

| Readout | Nature of floor | Positive control | Appraisal dimension detection |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Magnitude** (JS divergence of 13-emotion-word probability distribution) | **Higher than the effect** (F3/F4 approximately twice F1) | **Not reached in 3 of 4 models** | 0–2 out of 17 dimensions |
| **Direction** (projecting probability change onto human-rating-derived contrast) | **Zero-centered** (−0.005 to +0.008, 4 models) | Reached in some models | **+1.8 to +2.9 SD as a family** |

> **As long as the readout is magnitude, appraisal dimension directions cannot be distinguished from variance-matched random directions. When direction is measured, a family-level effect appears.**

**This is the central claim of this study.** What Tak et al. 2025 (Findings of ACL 2025) reported — "the emotion of the output moves as expected theoretically when an appraisal dimension is intervened upon" — was an **observation under a full-space floor and a magnitude readout.** This study **separates what the effect derives from and what it does not derive from by placing an in-plane, variance-matched floor and measuring direction.**

**This is not a denial of prior work.** The effect exists (exceeds the floor as a family). **This is positioned as the identification of the conditions under which the effect is identified.**

**This comparison is describable from the data in hand and requires no new judgements.** The 4 discovery set models are labeled exploratory.

## BM2. Registration of a Dose–Response Test (New)

The signed readout has been run only at $J^\*=0.05$. **If the effect increases monotonically with the perturbation budget, this constitutes independent evidence that the intervention is working.** Since random directions are unrelated to the contrast, the floor should remain zero-centered even when the budget is raised.

**Procedure**: The signed readout is measured at $J^\* \in \{0.02, 0.05, 0.10\}$. The number of floor trials, number of stimuli, $R^2$ threshold for adopting dimensions, and antisymmetrization are all unchanged.

**Prediction D1 (registered before seeing results)**:

1. **The family-level effect $\bar S$ increases monotonically with $J^\*$** (0.02 < 0.05 < 0.10)
2. **The floor mean is zero-centered at every budget** (|mean| < 2 SE)
3. At each budget, $\bar S >$ null p95

**Judgement D1**: All 3 conditions are met for the majority of the models tested.

**What can be written if it fails**:
- If not monotone: "The effect depends on the budget and does not show a monotonic dose–response." **Non-monotonicity or saturation is a sign that the intervention is too strong and is destroying representations**, and a breakdown at $J^\*=0.10$ is reported
- If the floor is not zero-centered: **No judgement is made at that budget**

## BM3. Execution Order (Resource Constraints)

Running 3 budgets for all models requires approximately 4–6 hours per model, which is not feasible.

- **Completing the confirmation set judgements ($J^\*=0.05$) is the top priority** (pass condition in Amendment 60)
- The dose–response test is run on **the 2 models with the strongest and weakest effects** (selected once the $J^\*=0.05$ results are complete; **the selection criterion is fixed here**: the models with the maximum and minimum $\bar S$)
- **The reason for the selection and the fact that other models were not tested are explicitly stated**

## BM4. Hierarchy of Claims (The Finalized Way of Writing)

| Judgement passed | What can be written |
|---|---|
| Aggregate test (majority of confirmation set) | An intervention on appraisal concept directions moves the emotion output, **as a family**, in the direction predicted by human ratings. Holds under a variance-matched in-plane floor |
| + Dose–response D1 | That effect **increases monotonically with perturbation amount**. Responds to intervention strength |
| + X2′ | Appraisal concepts are **upstream** of emotion |
| + X4 | The causal power of emotion directions is **carried only by the component along general axes** |

**What is not written** (finalized):
- Individual evaluation dimensions control emotion (on the same order as the floor tail, Amendment 62)
- Emotion is exhausted by appraisal concepts (X1, not held for 10 models)
- There are shared representations of emotion families across models (M1′ not held)
- Concepts not conceptualized by humans (excluded from targets in Amendment 46)
